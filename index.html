The Garden Plan

How to use this plan

Seven beds wrap the house, each planted from a Cape Cod native palette. Open any bed to see its plants, rearrange them, try a different look, and preview how the bed will stand up in real life — then save your work or print a report.

Getting started

  • Tap a bed on the plan to open its planting tray beside the drawing.
  • The tray shows the bed from above (the schematic), an elevation view below it, and the list of plants growing there.
  • Tap the × at the top of the tray, or another bed, to close it.

Arranging plants

  • Move a plant — drag its token around the schematic. In the two shared beds, plants move freely between both halves.
  • Remove a plant — hover or tap a token and click the ✕ that appears on it.
  • Add a plant — use ＋ Add a plant to open the palette, then pick from the Cape Cod natives that suit the bed.
  • Swap a plant by removing one and adding another. Tap any token to see its details — light, height, pollinators, and care links — and to set bloom color where offered.

Suggested designs

  • Each bed comes with a hand-curated Designer's Pick, plus a few alternate styles that fit the bed's light and soil.
  • Under Suggested designs, tap a style chip to lay that design into the bed straight away.
  • Changed your mind after moving things around? Reset to suggestion rebuilds the currently selected style.

The elevation view

  • The elevation shows the bed at eye level — how the plants' heights, shapes, and blooms read side by side, even out of season.
  • Drag the elevation to spin the viewpoint around the bed and tilt it up or down; the arrow keys do the same.
  • Or use 📍 Set viewpoint to drop a spot on the plan and look at the bed from exactly there.

Saving & reports

  • 💾 Save this bed stores a single bed's planting as a reusable design you can reload into that bed any time.
  • 🗂️ Saved plans keeps whole-plan saves and your single-bed designs together, ready to reload.
  • 📋 Make report builds a printable guide — the full plan or just the beds you choose.

The plant palette

Every plant here is chosen for the Cape: native, drought- and salt-tolerant, and pollinator-friendly — “right plant, right place.” The garden feeds bees, butterflies, and birds while protecting groundwater and the bays.

Each bed follows classic design rules so it reads well all year: plants are grouped in odd-numbered drifts (the Rule of Three — 3, 5, or 7, never lonely singles), those drifts repeat across the bed for cohesion, and varied foliage textures are mixed so a bed still looks good out of bloom.
